About this game
IMPOSTER is the ultimate party social deduction game for infinite players. One player secretly becomes the Imposter — they don't know the secret word. Everyone else does.

Give voice clues, discuss, debate, and vote. Can the group sniff out the imposter before they blend in perfectly? Can the imposter figure out the word just from everyone else's hints?

No apps to install. No internet needed. Just phones, friends, and deception. Perfect for parties, travel, family nights, and icebreakers. Features 100+ words across Animals, Foods, Objects, Professions, and Nature categories — no two rounds are ever the same.
